THE RED PLANET
Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and one of the most extensively explored worlds beyond Earth. Its reddish appearance comes mainly from iron minerals in the surface rocks that have oxidized, essentially forming rust. Mars has a thin atmosphere dominated by carbon dioxide and a surface covered with ancient craters, volcanoes, valleys and vast dusty plains. The planet is home to Olympus Mons, the largest known volcano in the Solar System, and Valles Marineris, one of the largest canyon systems known. Mars also has two small natural satellites: Phobos and Deimos.
OLYMPUS MONS
Olympus Mons is the largest known volcano in the Solar System and rises roughly 22 km above the surrounding plains.
VALLES MARINERIS
A gigantic canyon system stretching thousands of kilometres across the Martian surface.
TWO MOONS
Mars has two irregular natural satellites: Phobos, the larger and closer moon, and Deimos, the smaller and more distant one.
DID YOU KNOW?
A Martian day is called a sol and lasts about 24 hours 39 minutes.
