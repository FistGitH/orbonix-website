MARS'S INNER MOON
Phobos is the larger and closer of the two natural satellites of Mars. It is an irregular, heavily cratered body with a surface dominated by impact debris and grooves. Phobos orbits Mars extremely quickly. It completes one orbit in only about 7 hours and 39 minutes, meaning that it travels around Mars faster than Mars rotates. From the surface of Mars, Phobos would appear to rise in the west and set in the east. Scientists believe Phobos may be a captured asteroid or could have formed from material ejected by a massive impact on Mars.
RAPID ORBIT
Phobos completes an orbit around Mars in less than 8 hours.
STICKNEY CRATER
The enormous Stickney crater is the largest impact feature on Phobos.
DOOMED MOON
Phobos is slowly spiraling toward Mars and is expected to eventually break apart or impact the planet.
DID YOU KNOW?
Phobos orbits Mars so quickly that it can rise and set more than once in a single Martian day.
