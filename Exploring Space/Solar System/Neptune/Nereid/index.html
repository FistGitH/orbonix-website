THE ECCENTRIC MOON
Nereid is one of Neptune's outer moons and one of the most unusual objects in the Neptune system. It was discovered in 1949 by astronomer Gerard Kuiper and was the second moon of Neptune to be discovered. Nereid has an extremely eccentric orbit. Its distance from Neptune changes dramatically during each revolution, taking it from relatively close to the planet to millions of kilometres away. Because of its unusual orbit and irregular shape, Nereid is thought to have a very different history from Neptune's regular inner moons. Voyager 2 photographed Nereid during its 1989 encounter with Neptune.
