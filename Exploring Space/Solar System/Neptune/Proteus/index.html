THE DARK MOON
Proteus is the second-largest moon of Neptune and one of the largest irregular satellites in the Solar System. It was discovered in images taken by Voyager 2 during its flyby of Neptune in 1989. Proteus has a dark surface and an irregular, heavily cratered shape. Its appearance suggests that it has experienced many impacts throughout its history. The moon is not perfectly spherical because its gravity is not strong enough to pull its material into a rounded shape. Proteus orbits Neptune relatively close to the planet and completes one orbit in just over one Earth day.
PROTEUS FEATURES
HEAVILY CRATERED
Proteus has a surface covered with impact craters created by collisions with other objects.
IRREGULAR SHAPE
Its gravity is not strong enough to make the moon spherical, giving Proteus its distinctive irregular appearance.
VOYAGER 2
Proteus was discovered when Voyager 2 photographed the Neptune system during its historic 1989 encounter.
DID YOU KNOW?
Proteus is larger than some spherical moons, but it remains irregularly shaped because its gravity is too weak to pull it into a sphere.
