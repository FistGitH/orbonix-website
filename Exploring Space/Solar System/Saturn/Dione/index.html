THE ICY MOON
Dione is one of Saturn's major icy moons. Its surface is dominated by water ice and is covered with impact craters. One of its most recognizable features is a network of bright fractures called wispy terrain. These huge cliffs and fractures can stretch for hundreds of kilometers across the surface. Dione is also tidally locked to Saturn, meaning that the same hemisphere always faces the planet. Scientists have also found evidence that Dione may contain a subsurface ocean beneath its icy crust.
