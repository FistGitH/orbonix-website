THE COMPANION OF ORCUS
Vanth is the natural satellite of the dwarf planet Orcus, located in the distant Kuiper Belt. It was discovered in 2005 by astronomer Mike Brown and his team using observations from the Hubble Space Telescope. Vanth is unusually large compared with its parent body. This makes the Orcus–Vanth system more similar to a binary system than a typical planet–moon pair. Vanth orbits Orcus at a distance of roughly 9,000 kilometres and completes one revolution in about 9.5 Earth days. The surface composition and origin of Vanth are still not completely known. Scientists believe it may contain water ice and other frozen materials.
