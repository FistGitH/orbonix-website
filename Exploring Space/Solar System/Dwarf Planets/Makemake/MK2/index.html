THE DARK COMPANION
MK2 is the small natural satellite of the dwarf planet Makemake. It was discovered in observations made with the Hubble Space Telescope and announced in 2016. The moon is officially designated S/2015 (136472) 1 and is commonly referred to as MK2. MK2 is much darker than Makemake. Its dark surface makes it difficult to observe directly from Earth, which is one reason its discovery took so long. The moon's orbit around Makemake helps scientists study the mass and density of the dwarf planet.
MK2 FEATURES
HUBBLE DISCOVERY
MK2 was identified using images from the Hubble Space Telescope in the outer Solar System.
VERY DARK
MK2 reflects very little sunlight, making it significantly harder to detect than Makemake itself.
MAKEMAKE'S MOON
MK2 is currently the only known natural satellite of the dwarf planet Makemake.
DID YOU KNOW?
MK2 is so dark that scientists had difficulty detecting it next to the much brighter Makemake.
