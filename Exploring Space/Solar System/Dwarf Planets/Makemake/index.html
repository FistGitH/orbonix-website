THE FROZEN WORLD
Makemake is a dwarf planet located in the Kuiper Belt beyond the orbit of Neptune. It was discovered in 2005 and is one of the largest known objects in the outer Solar System. Makemake has a reddish-brown surface covered with frozen materials, including methane and nitrogen ices. The dwarf planet is extremely cold and takes more than three centuries to complete one orbit around the Sun. Makemake also has a small known moon, officially designated S/2015 (136472) 1 and commonly nicknamed MK2.
MAKEMAKE'S MOON
MK2
MK2 is the small moon of Makemake. It was discovered in 2016 from observations made with the Hubble Space Telescope. Its official designation is S/2015 (136472) 1. MK2 is much darker than Makemake and is estimated to be roughly 175 km across.
