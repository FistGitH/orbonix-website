ERIS

A distant dwarf planet beyond Neptune, one of the most massive known objects in the scattered disc and a world that helped redefine the Solar System.
THE DISTANT WORLD
Eris is a dwarf planet located far beyond Neptune in a region of the Solar System known as the scattered disc. It was discovered in 2005 by a team led by astronomer Mike Brown. Its discovery was one of the major events that led to the reclassification of Pluto as a dwarf planet in 2006. Eris is slightly smaller in diameter than Pluto, but its mass is slightly greater. It has an extremely distant and elongated orbit around the Sun. Eris takes more than five centuries to complete one orbit. The dwarf planet has one known moon, Dysnomia, which helped astronomers determine Eris's mass.
