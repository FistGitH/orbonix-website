SMALL WORLDS · BIG STORIES
Dwarf planets are worlds that orbit the Sun and are massive enough for their gravity to make them approximately round, but they have not cleared their orbital neighbourhoods. The five officially recognized dwarf planets are Ceres, Pluto, Eris, Haumea and Makemake. There are also several large trans-Neptunian objects that are considered strong dwarf-planet candidates, including Orcus and Gonggong. Explore each world above to learn about its size, orbit, composition, discovery and place in the Solar System.
DID YOU KNOW?
There may be many more dwarf planets hidden in the distant outer Solar System.
