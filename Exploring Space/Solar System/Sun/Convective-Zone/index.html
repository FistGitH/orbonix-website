WHAT IS THE CONVECTIVE ZONE?
The convective zone is the outer region of the Sun's interior, extending from roughly 70% of the Sun's radius all the way to the visible surface. Here, the plasma becomes sufficiently opaque that radiation alone is no longer an efficient way to transport energy. Instead, energy is carried by large-scale motions of the plasma. Hot plasma rises toward the surface. As it loses energy, it becomes cooler and denser and eventually sinks again. This creates enormous convection currents that continuously transport energy outward.
