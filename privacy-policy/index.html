Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Orbonix handles information when you use the Orbonix website, account features, Facebook Page integration, automation tools, and related services.

Last updated: October 4, 2026

1. Information we may process

Depending on the feature you use, Orbonix may process information you provide directly, such as account details, preferences, messages, or content submitted through the website. Technical information may also be processed when needed to operate, secure, and improve the service.

2. Facebook and Meta integration

Orbonix may connect to Facebook Pages through Meta APIs and Webhooks in order to detect new content published on an Orbonix-managed Facebook Page and automate distribution of that content to connected services.

Depending on the permissions granted to the Orbonix Meta application, the integration may process limited Page information such as Page identifiers, post identifiers, post text, timestamps, permalink URLs, media metadata, and other Page content required to perform the requested automation.

Orbonix does not request or store your Facebook password. Authentication and authorization are handled through Meta's own systems.

3. How Facebook data is used

  • To identify the authorized Orbonix Facebook Page.
  • To receive webhook notifications when relevant Page content is created or changed.
  • To retrieve the content of authorized Page posts for automation.
  • To send authorized content to connected services such as social networks or community channels.
  • To diagnose errors, prevent abuse, and maintain service reliability.

4. Data sharing

Orbonix does not sell personal information. When an automation is enabled, content from the authorized Facebook Page may be transmitted to service providers or connected platforms only as necessary to perform the requested action. Those services process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Service providers

Orbonix may rely on infrastructure, hosting, database, analytics, automation, and API providers to operate its services. These providers may process limited information only as necessary to provide their services.

6. Data retention

Orbonix aims to retain integration data only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the relevant feature, maintain security, diagnose errors, or meet legal obligations. Temporary identifiers, webhook event data, and access credentials may expire, be rotated, or be deleted when no longer required.

7. Access tokens and credentials

Access tokens, app secrets, API keys, and similar credentials are treated as confidential technical information. They are not intentionally exposed on public web pages. Where integrations require such credentials, they should be stored in secure server-side or platform-managed environments.

8. Your choices and revocation

Access granted to the Orbonix Meta application can be revoked through Facebook or Meta account settings where available. If access is revoked, Orbonix automation features that depend on that access may stop working.

9. Data deletion requests

You may request deletion of information associated with Orbonix integrations through the instructions on the Data Deletion page. You may also revoke the Orbonix application's access through Meta where supported.

10. Security

Orbonix uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect their own accounts and credentials.

11. Children's privacy

Orbonix does not knowingly use its integrations to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law or platform rules. Access to third-party services remains subject to those services' own age and consent requirements.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Orbonix features, integrations, legal requirements, or service providers change. The current version will be published on this page with an updated date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the official Orbonix contact channels available on the Orbonix website.

This policy is intended to describe the actual Orbonix website and Facebook/Meta automation workflow. It should remain consistent with the permissions and data flows that are enabled in production.
