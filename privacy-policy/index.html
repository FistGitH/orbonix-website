Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Orbonix handles information when you use the Orbonix website, account features, integrations, and related services.

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Information we may process

Depending on the feature you use, Orbonix may process information you provide directly, such as account details, messages, preferences, or content submitted through the website. Technical information may also be processed when needed to operate, secure, and improve the service.

2. TikTok integration

If you choose to connect TikTok, Orbonix may use TikTok Login Kit and the TikTok Content Posting API. The integration may receive basic account information authorized by you and authentication credentials such as access or refresh tokens. These credentials are used only to authenticate the connected account and perform actions you explicitly authorize, such as publishing eligible Orbonix video content.

Orbonix does not request your TikTok password. TikTok authentication is handled through TikTok's own authorization flow.

3. How information is used

  • To provide website and account functionality.
  • To authenticate connected services when you request an integration.
  • To publish or manage content only when that action has been authorized.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, diagnose errors, and improve reliability.

4. Service providers

Orbonix may rely on infrastructure, hosting, analytics, automation, database, or API providers to operate its services. These providers may process limited information only as necessary to provide their services and according to their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Data sharing and sale

Orbonix does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate a feature, comply with applicable law, protect the service, or fulfill an action you requested.

6. Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant feature, security, legal obligations, or service operation. Connected-service credentials may be removed or invalidated when an integration is disconnected, revoked, expires, or is no longer required.

7. Your choices

You can revoke access granted to third-party services through those services' account settings where available. You may also stop using an Orbonix integration at any time. Requests concerning Orbonix-held information can be made through the official Orbonix contact channels shown on the website.

8. Security

Orbonix uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect their own accounts and credentials.

9. Children's privacy

Orbonix does not knowingly use integrations to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law or platform rules. Access to third-party services remains subject to the age requirements and parental-consent rules of those services and the user's jurisdiction.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Orbonix features, integrations, legal requirements, or service providers change. The current version will be published on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the official Orbonix contact channels available on the Orbonix website.

This page describes Orbonix's intended data practices and should be kept consistent with the services and integrations actually enabled on the site.
