THE JOURNEY BEYOND THE PLANETS
The Voyager program consists of two robotic spacecraft, Voyager 1 and Voyager 2, launched in 1977 to explore the outer Solar System. Voyager 1 visited Jupiter and Saturn before continuing on a trajectory out of the Solar System. Voyager 2 made the first and only close flybys of Uranus and Neptune. Both spacecraft used gravity-assist maneuvers to gain speed by passing close to planets. After completing their planetary missions, the spacecraft continued into the outer heliosphere and eventually entered interstellar space. Voyager 1 became the first human-made object to enter interstellar space, followed by Voyager 2. Even after decades in space, the spacecraft continue to provide unique measurements of the environment far beyond the planets.
VOYAGER FEATURES
VOYAGER 1
Voyager 1 explored Jupiter and Saturn and is now traveling through interstellar space farther from Earth than any other human-made spacecraft.
VOYAGER 2
Voyager 2 is the only spacecraft to have visited all four giant planets: Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune.
GOLDEN RECORD
Each Voyager carries a golden record containing sounds, images and greetings representing life and civilization on Earth.
DID YOU KNOW?
The Voyager spacecraft are traveling so far from Earth that their radio signals take many hours to reach us.
