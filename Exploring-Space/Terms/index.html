SPACE TERMS

A collection of important astronomical terms used throughout the Orbonix space explorer. Learn the language of the Universe.
01 · DISTANCE

ASTRONOMICAL UNIT

An astronomical unit, or AU, is the average distance between Earth and the Sun. 1 AU is about 149.6 million kilometers.
02 · MOTION

ORBIT

An orbit is the curved path an object follows around another object because of gravity.
03 · FORCE

GRAVITY

Gravity is the force that attracts objects with mass toward one another.
04 · PLANETS

ATMOSPHERE

An atmosphere is a layer of gases surrounding a planet, moon, or other astronomical object.
05 · MOONS

SATELLITE

A satellite is an object that orbits another object. Natural satellites include moons.
06 · PLANET CLASS

DWARF PLANET

A dwarf planet orbits the Sun and is nearly round, but has not cleared its orbital region.
07 · SMALL BODIES

ASTEROID

An asteroid is a rocky or metallic body that orbits the Sun.
08 · SMALL BODIES

COMET

A comet is an icy body that can develop a glowing coma and tail when it approaches the Sun.
09 · SOLAR SYSTEM

KUIPER BELT

The Kuiper Belt is a distant region beyond Neptune containing icy bodies and dwarf planets.
10 · OUTER SOLAR SYSTEM

OORT CLOUD

The Oort Cloud is a theoretical enormous shell of icy objects surrounding the Solar System.
11 · DISTANCE

LIGHT-YEAR

A light-year is the distance light travels through space in one year: about 9.46 trillion km.
12 · DEEP SPACE

NEBULA

A nebula is a huge cloud of gas and dust in space. Some are regions where stars form.
13 · GALACTIC STRUCTURE

GALAXY

A galaxy is a massive system of stars, gas, dust, dark matter, and other objects held together by gravity.
14 · EXTREME OBJECTS

BLACK HOLE

A black hole is a region of spacetime where gravity is so strong that nothing, including light, can escape from inside the event horizon.
15 · BLACK HOLES

EVENT HORIZON

The event horizon is the boundary around a black hole beyond which escape is impossible.
16 · BLACK HOLES

SINGULARITY

A singularity is the theoretical central region of a classical black-hole model where spacetime curvature becomes extreme.
17 · OTHER WORLDS

EXOPLANET

An exoplanet is a planet that orbits a star outside our Solar System.
18 · STELLAR DEATH

SUPERNOVA

A supernova is an extremely powerful stellar explosion that can briefly outshine an entire galaxy.
19 · STELLAR REMNANTS

NEUTRON STAR

A neutron star is the incredibly dense remnant left after certain massive stars explode.
20 · STELLAR REMNANTS

WHITE DWARF

A white dwarf is the hot, dense remnant of a Sun-like star after it has shed its outer layers.
21 · COSMOLOGY

DARK MATTER

Dark matter is invisible matter inferred from its gravitational effects on visible matter and cosmic structures.
22 · COSMOLOGY

DARK ENERGY

Dark energy is the name given to whatever is driving the accelerated expansion of the Universe.
23 · THE SUN

SOLAR WIND

Solar wind is a continuous flow of charged particles released by the Sun into space.
24 · SOLAR SYSTEM

HELIOSPHERE

The heliosphere is the enormous bubble around the Sun created by the solar wind.
25 · SOLAR SYSTEM

HELIOPAUSE

The heliopause is the outer boundary of the heliosphere where the solar wind meets interstellar space.
26 · DISTANCE

PARSEC

A parsec is an astronomical distance unit equal to about 3.26 light-years.
27 · STARS

RED GIANT

A red giant is an evolved star that has expanded enormously after exhausting hydrogen in its core.
28 · STARS

MAIN SEQUENCE

The main sequence is the stage where a star fuses hydrogen into helium in its core.
29 · PLANETS

HABITABLE ZONE

The habitable zone is the region around a star where temperatures could allow liquid water on a planet's surface.
30 · GRAVITY

ROCHE LIMIT

The Roche limit is the distance within which tidal forces can break apart a smaller object held together mainly by gravity.
31 · GRAVITY

TIDAL FORCES

Tidal forces are differences in gravitational pull across an object that can stretch or deform it.
