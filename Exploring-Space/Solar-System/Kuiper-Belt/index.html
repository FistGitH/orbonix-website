THE ICY FRONTIER
The Kuiper Belt is a vast region of the Solar System beyond the orbit of Neptune. It contains millions of icy bodies, including dwarf planets, comets and other trans-Neptunian objects. Pluto is one of the most famous objects in the Kuiper Belt. Other important worlds include Eris, Haumea, Makemake and Quaoar. The objects of the Kuiper Belt are made largely of frozen materials such as water ice, methane and ammonia, mixed with rock and complex organic compounds. Because these objects formed far from the Sun and have remained extremely cold, many preserve material from the early Solar System. The Kuiper Belt is also an important source of short-period comets that can enter the inner Solar System.
