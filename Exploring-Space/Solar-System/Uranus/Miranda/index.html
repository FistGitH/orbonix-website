THE STRANGE MOON
Miranda is the smallest of Uranus' five major moons, but its surface is one of the most unusual in the Solar System. It contains enormous cliffs, deep canyons, impact craters and strange regions of terrain that look very different from one another. The most famous feature is Verona Rupes, a gigantic cliff that may rise several kilometres above the surrounding terrain. Scientists think Miranda experienced significant geological activity in its past. Tidal interactions with Uranus may have provided energy that helped reshape its surface.
