THE SMALLEST PLANET
Mercury is the smallest planet in the Solar System and the closest planet to the Sun. Its surface is heavily cratered and resembles the Moon. Mercury has only an extremely thin exosphere rather than a substantial atmosphere. Mercury also has a very unusual rotation. It rotates once every 58.6 Earth days and orbits the Sun every 88 Earth days. Because of its 3:2 spin-orbit resonance, one solar day on Mercury lasts about 176 Earth days.
EXTREME TEMPERATURES
Mercury experiences enormous temperature differences between its day and night sides.
GIANT IRON CORE
Mercury has a proportionally enormous metallic core compared with the other terrestrial planets.
WATER ICE
Permanent shadows inside some deep polar craters can remain cold enough to preserve water ice.
DID YOU KNOW?
A single day from sunrise to sunrise on Mercury lasts about 176 Earth days.
