THE DEATH STAR MOON
Mimas is one of Saturn's smaller major moons and is composed primarily of water ice mixed with rocky material. Its most famous feature is Herschel Crater, a gigantic impact crater approximately 130 kilometers wide. The crater covers a substantial portion of Mimas' surface and gives the moon its famous resemblance to the fictional Death Star. Mimas is also tidally locked to Saturn, meaning the same side of the moon always faces the planet.
HERSCHEL CRATER
The enormous Herschel Crater is about one-third the diameter of Mimas itself.
ICY WORLD
Mimas is dominated by water ice, giving its surface a bright appearance.
TIDALLY LOCKED
Mimas rotates once for every orbit around Saturn, keeping one hemisphere facing the planet.
DID YOU KNOW?
Mimas' enormous Herschel Crater makes the moon look remarkably similar to the Death Star.
