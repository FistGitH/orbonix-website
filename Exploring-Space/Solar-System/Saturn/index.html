Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and the second-largest planet in the Solar System. Its spectacular ring system is made mostly of countless particles of water ice, rocky material and dust. RING SYSTEM
Saturn's rings are made primarily of water ice particles mixed with rock and dust. The main rings extend hundreds of thousands of kilometres from the planet.
TITAN
Titan is Saturn's largest moon and has a thick atmosphere. It is the only moon known to have stable lakes and seas of liquid hydrocarbons on its surface.
