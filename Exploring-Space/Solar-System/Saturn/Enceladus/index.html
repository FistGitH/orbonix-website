A HIDDEN OCEAN WORLD
Enceladus is one of the most intriguing moons in the Solar System. Its surface is covered by extremely bright water ice and is one of the most reflective surfaces known in the Solar System. Beneath the icy crust lies a global ocean of liquid water. The Cassini spacecraft discovered enormous jets erupting from fractures near the moon's south pole. These plumes contain water vapor, ice particles and organic compounds. Because liquid water, energy and organic molecules are present, Enceladus is one of the most important places in the Solar System in the search for potentially habitable environments.
SUBSURFACE OCEAN
A global ocean of liquid water exists beneath Enceladus' icy crust.
ICE PLUMES
Powerful jets eject water vapor and ice particles hundreds of kilometers above the surface.
ORGANIC MOLECULES
Cassini detected organic compounds in material originating from the subsurface ocean.
DID YOU KNOW?
Enceladus continuously sprays material from its hidden ocean into space through giant ice geysers.
