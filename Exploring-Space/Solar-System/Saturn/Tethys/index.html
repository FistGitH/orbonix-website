THE ICY MOON
Tethys is one of Saturn's larger icy moons. Its surface is dominated by water ice and is heavily covered with impact craters. The most spectacular feature on Tethys is Odysseus, an enormous impact crater roughly 450 kilometers across. Another remarkable feature is Ithaca Chasma, a huge canyon system that stretches for hundreds of kilometers across the moon. Tethys is tidally locked to Saturn, so the same side of the moon always faces the planet.
ODYSSEUS CRATER
A gigantic impact basin nearly 450 km wide dominates one side of Tethys.
ITHACA CHASMA
A vast system of cliffs and valleys extends across a large part of Tethys.
WATER ICE
Tethys has a very low density, indicating that it contains a large amount of water ice.
DID YOU KNOW?
The gigantic Odysseus crater covers almost two-fifths of Tethys' diameter.
