THE SEARCH FOR A HIDDEN WORLD
Planet X is a hypothetical planet that may exist far beyond Neptune. The modern Planet X hypothesis is closely related to the idea of "Planet Nine". Researchers proposed that the unusual orbits of several distant trans-Neptunian objects could be explained by the gravity of a large, currently undiscovered planet. The proposed object would be extremely far from the Sun. Because of this enormous distance, it would receive very little sunlight and would be exceptionally faint from Earth. Scientists have not directly detected Planet Nine, and its existence remains unconfirmed. The search continues using powerful ground-based telescopes and astronomical surveys.

NOT DISCOVERED
No telescope has yet produced a confirmed direct observation of the proposed planet.
