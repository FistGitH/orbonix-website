THE MOON OF GONGGONG
Xiangliu is the only known natural satellite of the dwarf planet Gonggong. It was discovered in 2016 in images obtained with the Hubble Space Telescope. The discovery allowed astronomers to study the Gonggong system in greater detail. Xiangliu is much smaller than Gonggong and orbits its parent body at a distance of roughly fifteen thousand kilometres. The moon is named after Xiangliu, a nine-headed poisonous snake-like monster from Chinese mythology associated with the legendary flood story. Because Gonggong and Xiangliu are located extremely far from the Sun, they receive very little sunlight and remain among the coldest and darkest worlds known in the Solar System.
