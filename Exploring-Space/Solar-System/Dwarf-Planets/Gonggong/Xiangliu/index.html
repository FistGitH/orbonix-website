THE MOON OF GONGGONG
Xiang Liu is the known natural satellite of Gonggong, a large trans-Neptunian object in the distant Kuiper Belt. The moon was discovered in images obtained with the Hubble Space Telescope and its discovery was announced in 2016. Xiang Liu is much smaller than Gonggong and is difficult to observe directly because the entire system is extremely distant from Earth and receives very little sunlight. Studying the orbit of Xiang Liu helps astronomers determine the mass and other physical properties of the Gonggong system. The moon is named after Xiangliu, a nine-headed serpent-like creature from ancient Chinese mythology. Gonggong and Xiang Liu belong to one of the most remote planetary systems known in the Solar System, far beyond Neptune.
