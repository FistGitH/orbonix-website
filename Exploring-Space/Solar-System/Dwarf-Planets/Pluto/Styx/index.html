STYX

A tiny, irregular moon of Pluto, orbiting within the complex gravitational system of Pluto and Charon.
THE TINY MOON
Styx is one of Pluto's five known moons. It was discovered in 2012 using the Hubble Space Telescope. The moon is extremely small and has an irregular shape. Its name comes from the River Styx in Greek mythology, the river that separated the world of the living from the realm of the dead. Styx orbits Pluto between the orbits of Charon and Nix. The moon is part of a remarkably complex system in which Pluto and Charon orbit a common centre of mass, while the smaller moons travel around both bodies. New Horizons observed the Pluto system during its historic flyby in 2015, although Styx was too small and distant for detailed surface imaging.

STYX FEATURES
TINY WORLD
Styx is one of the smallest known moons in the Pluto system, measuring only a few kilometres across.
COMPLEX ORBIT
Styx travels around the Pluto-Charon system and is strongly affected by their combined gravitational field.
HUBBLE
Styx was discovered in 2012 in observations made with the Hubble Space Telescope.
DID YOU KNOW?
Styx is so small that its estimated size is only about 16 kilometres along its longest dimension.
