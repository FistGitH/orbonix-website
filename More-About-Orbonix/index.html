ORBONIX · ABOUT THE PROJECT

ORBONIX

A growing interactive universe dedicated to space, astronomy, exploration and scientific discovery.
01 · WHAT IS ORBONIX?
A UNIVERSE OF KNOWLEDGE
ORBONIX is a space exploration project built around one simple idea: make the universe easier to explore.

Instead of putting astronomy information into one giant collection of text, ORBONIX turns it into an interactive experience with visual pages, simulations, quizzes, observations and exploration.
02 · THE IDEA
EXPLORE. LEARN. DISCOVER.
ORBONIX is designed to feel less like a traditional educational website and more like entering a digital space observatory. You can travel through the Solar System, learn about planets and moons, discover deep-space objects and test your knowledge.
03 · ASTRONOMY
FROM THE SUN TO DEEP SPACE
The project covers everything from the Sun, planets, dwarf planets and moons to galaxies, nebulae, quasars, black holes, dark matter, dark energy, cosmic voids and other extreme objects.
04 · INTERACTION
MORE THAN INFORMATION
ORBONIX is constantly moving toward interactive astronomy. Pages contain animations, realistic visuals, quizzes, observation content and eventually increasingly detailed space simulations.
THE ORBONIX MISSION

MAKE SPACE FEEL EXPLORABLE

The goal of ORBONIX is not simply to tell people facts about the universe. The goal is to let them explore it. From standing on the surface of a moon to travelling between planets, from learning how a black hole works to recognising a galaxy in the night sky — ORBONIX is being built as a place where curiosity becomes exploration.

WHAT EXISTS INSIDE ORBONIX
☀️
SOLAR SYSTEM
A growing collection covering the Sun, eight planets, dwarf planets and the planetary environment.
🌙
MOONS
Detailed exploration of natural satellites, including dedicated pages for important moons such as Ganymede and Callisto.
🌌
DEEP SPACE
Galaxies, nebulae, quasars, black holes, wormholes, cosmic voids, dark matter and dark energy.
🧠
SPACE KNOWLEDGE
Interactive quizzes designed to test astronomy knowledge from beginner questions to much harder challenges.
🔭
OBSERVATION
Astronomical observations, telescope targets, astrophotography and guides for exploring the real night sky.
🛰️
SIMULATION
A realistic Solar System simulation with real distances, planets, dwarf planets, moons and interactive camera navigation.
THE FUTURE OF ORBONIX

FROM WEBSITE TO DIGITAL UNIVERSE

ORBONIX is still being developed. The project is gradually expanding from a collection of astronomy pages into a larger interactive space environment. More objects, more simulations, more observations, more knowledge and more ways to explore the universe will continue to be added.
