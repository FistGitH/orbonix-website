FROM AN IDEA TO A SPACE UNIVERSE
ORBONIX started as a simple idea about space exploration. It has grown into an interactive space platform with planets, moons, deep-space objects, quizzes, observations and simulations. This page records the major milestones we have achieved along the way.
BEGINNING
🚀
ORBONIX WAS CREATED
The ORBONIX space project began with the idea of creating a place where anyone could explore and learn about the universe.
FIRST MILESTONE
SOLAR SYSTEM
☀️
SOLAR SYSTEM COMPLETED
We created a dedicated Solar System section containing the Sun, planets, dwarf planets and information about our planetary neighbourhood.
SOLAR SYSTEM
PLANETS
🪐
PLANET EXPLORATION
Individual pages were created for the major planets, with detailed information, visuals, scientific parameters and interactive elements.

MOONS

THE MOON SYSTEM EXPANDED
We began building detailed pages for natural satellites, including the major moons of Jupiter such as Ganymede and Callisto.

DEEP SPACE

DEEP SPACE WAS BORN
The website expanded beyond the Solar System with sections dedicated to galaxies, nebulae, quasars, black holes, dark matter, dark energy and other extreme cosmic objects.
DEEP SPACE
KNOWLEDGE

Interactive astronomy quizzes were created with questions ranging from basic space knowledge to much harder scientific concepts.
SPACE KNOWLEDGE
OBSERVATION

ASTRONOMY OBSERVATION
